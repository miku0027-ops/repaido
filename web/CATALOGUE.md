# Sample service catalogue

19 illustrative services across 10 categories. All prices, durations and scopes are prototype data, not provider quotes or confirmed availability. Car services are independent sample listings; the Auto Singar advertisement does not establish a provider partnership. Existing cart, quantity, details and sample checkout consume the same service records.

Category inspiration: Urban Company's public service directory (https://www.urbancompany.com/near-me/) and NoBroker's service listings (https://www.nobroker.in/cleaning-services). Original descriptions and sample prices are used; competitor branding, claims and pricing are not copied. Car washing and repair were added at the user's request.

Backend/provider onboarding, live availability and real payments remain outside this preview integration. Survey and inspection fees exclude the quoted work, as stated on each card and detail panel.
