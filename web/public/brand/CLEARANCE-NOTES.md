# Preliminary brand conflict review — 17 September 2026

Status: NOT LEGALLY CLEARED. India assumed as initial market. This is an initial public-web review, not a trademark availability opinion or comprehensive copyright review.

## Findings
- Searches for “Repaido”, “Repaido India”, and “Repaido” with repair/app/trademark terms did not surface a clear exact-name business match in the returned results. This does not establish availability; no authenticated registry search was completed.
- The house-and-checkmark concept already exists in stock artwork. Examples: https://www.vectorstock.com/royalty-free-vector/house-checkmark-logo-vector-47816731 and https://www.istockphoto.com/vector/check-home-vector-logo-design-template-logo-for-real-estate-business-gm1455310383-490683186 . These are concept-level leads, not findings that our precise paths are identical or infringing.
- A third-party record for HOUSE CHECK (US serial 88346912, registration 6066772) describes a house outline with a check mark, for home inspection services: https://trademarks.justia.com/883/46/house-88346912.html . Current legal status and territorial relevance were not verified in the official US registry. This is a review lead, not a conclusion about Indian rights.
- Rapido On Demand advertises plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, and cleaning: https://www.rapidoondemand.com/services . The similar-sounding name merits phonetic review; Indian rights and market overlap were not established.
- The Indian official search portal https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/ presents CAPTCHA and OTP login. No credentials were supplied and no authenticated search was performed.

## What remains
Have an Indian trademark professional assess exact, phonetic, device and unregistered-use conflicts for the name, symbol and combined logo in the intended services and markets. Repair/cleaning services are a starting point in class 37; marketplace and software activities may require additional classes depending on the actual offering. Check device/image similarity and copyright independently; original drawing does not itself establish trademark availability. WIPO offers image similarity searching, but no uploaded-image search was performed here: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database .

Indian statutory reference: https://www.ipindia.gov.in/tm-act-1999 — section 11 addresses confusion with earlier marks for identical/similar goods or services.

Recommendation: Treat current artwork as a provisional design. A more distinctive symbol is advisable before commissioning a formal clearance search and launch materials. Redesign alone would not guarantee clearance. No app artwork was replaced or legal filing submitted during this review.
